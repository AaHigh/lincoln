Lincoln Pub Comedy Nights

A three-tier competition structure designed to fill the room, protect quality, and recover cost through door and bar.

Captured 13 October 2026 from the handshake with Aaron Gossett. Documentation only. No application code yet.

Three-Tier Structure

Numbers are defaults. They change night to night.

Tier 1 — Room Builder

7:00 until roughly 8:00

  • Hard cap 3 minutes
  • Fee $0 (or $1–5 token)
  • Outside paid ranking
  • Tips are pure gratuity

Tier 2 — Standard Paid

8:15–9:00

  • Fee scales with field size ($10–30)
  • Sets 4–6 minutes
  • Modest operator seed ($40–75)
  • Top 30% or top 2–3 paid

Tier 3 — Heavy Hitter

9:00–10:15

  • Higher fee ($40–75+)
  • Sets 7–10 minutes
  • Larger seed ($100–150)
  • Keeps the room past 9:30

Timeline defaults

  • Host arrival 7:00–7:30
  • First comic 8:00–8:15
  • Door cover begins 8:15
  • Heavy-hitter cutoff 9:00
  • Target close 10:30 (face-offs only if the room is still full)

Economics

Operator money is made on door cover plus bar, especially the 8:30–10:30 window. Seeds are marketing spend. Entry fees offset that spend.

Recovery: Comic fees cover the seed. Audience tips grow the competitive pool and rank the comics. Door and incremental bar sales are the real return.

Medium night example: 6 paid comics at ~$25 average, $75 seed, $200 tips. Competitive pool ~$425. Top places take a strong multiple. Operator near break-even on the seed before extra bar time.

Dead night: Acceptable once. After two soft nights, lower the seed or raise the minimum fee.

Heavy-hitter incentive: A $100–150 floor plus tips can return $150–250+ on a $50 entry. Transparent live pot lets strong comics decide whether to cross town or fly in.

Two-Item Minimum

Translated from the classic comedy-club two-drink minimum. Any two menu items count.

Before ~8:15 (Tier 1)

No minimum. Arrive, watch short sets, tip a dollar, leave.

Tier 2 (8:15–9:00)

Two-item minimum to occupy a seat. Soda, $5 cocktail, or appetizer all count.

Tier 3 (9:00–close)

Two-premium-item minimum to keep the seat. Higher-priced cocktail, full pour, or anything clearly above the $5 floor.

Night Adjustments

Tuesday

Strummer’s handoff. Delay Tier 3 to 9:00–9:15 so comics finishing there around 8:45 can walk over ready. Inherit their audience for the second half of the night.

Wednesday

Hard stop. Smaller seeds, lower fees. Emphasize home by 10:45.

Thursday

Higher traffic. Larger seeds, more aggressive fees. Peak bar window. Stricter quality filter.

Legal Notes

These are notes, not legal advice.

Skill must predominate. Audience judgment of comedy via tips is a skill metric under California’s dominant-factor test.

Prize versus wager. Safer structure: announce fixed floors funded by the house. Treat comic admission as a separate stage fee. Do not let entry fees directly form a variable pot the comics are wagering on.

Keep ranking method objective and pre-stated. No chance elements.
